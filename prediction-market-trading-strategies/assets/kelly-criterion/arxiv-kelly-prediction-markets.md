## Summary

Academic paper applying Kelly criterion to prediction markets using logarithmic utility. Studies risk/return adjustments to prices, shows mean beliefs differ from market prices, proposes modified payout structures. Uses Kullback-Leibler divergence to analyze effects of bias misjudgment and investment fraction miscalculation on portfolio growth rate. Abstract only available — full PDF not scraped.
