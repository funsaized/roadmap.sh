## Summary

LVR (Loss-vs-Rebalancing) is the core arbitrage metric — measuring value extracted by arbitrageurs from AMM stale prices. PM-AMM achieves uniform LVR making arbitrage costs predictable for LPs. Total expected LVR = V₀/2 over market lifetime.
