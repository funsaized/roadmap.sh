# Learning Paths: Prediction Market Trading Strategies

## Overview

Four learning paths optimized for different goals. All paths share the same beginner foundation but diverge based on depth and specialization.

---

## Path 1: Quick Start (8-12 weeks, ~150 hours)

**Goal:** Start making informed prediction market trades as quickly as possible. Covers enough theory to avoid major mistakes and develop a basic edge.

### Week 1-2: Probability Essentials (~15h)
- 3Blue1Brown Bayes series (1h)
- Calibrate Your Judgment: 100 questions (3h)
- Odds conversion drills (2h)
- Expected value calculations (2h)
- Read: Superforecasting ch. 1-4 (4h)
- Probability distribution basics (3h)

### Week 3-4: Platform & Market Mechanics (~15h)
- Create accounts on Kalshi + Polymarket (1h)
- Study contract types, order books, fees (4h)
- Place small trades on both platforms (3h)
- Read resolution criteria for 10 markets (2h)
- Build fee comparison spreadsheet (2h)
- Watch platform tutorial videos (3h)

### Week 5-6: Forecasting & Research (~20h)
- Read: Superforecasting ch. 5-12 (5h)
- Make 20+ forecasts on Metaculus (5h)
- Learn systematic research workflow (3h)
- Explore FRED, polling aggregators, weather data (3h)
- Practice Fermi decomposition on 10 markets (4h)

### Week 7-8: Pricing & Edge Detection (~20h)
- Fair value, implied probability, overround (5h)
- Build mispricing scanner spreadsheet (4h)
- Study favorite-longshot bias (3h)
- Time decay and volatility basics (4h)
- Identify 5 potential edge opportunities (4h)

### Week 9-10: Risk Management (~15h)
- Kelly criterion (full + fractional) (5h)
- Build position sizing calculator (3h)
- Risk of ruin calculations (3h)
- Develop personal bankroll rules (2h)
- Paper trade 10 positions with Kelly sizing (2h)

### Week 11-12: Live Trading (~15h)
- Execute 20+ real trades across 2 platforms (8h)
- Track all trades in a journal (3h)
- Calculate Brier score and P&L (2h)
- Review and adjust strategy (2h)

**Outcome:** Can identify mispriced contracts, size positions with Kelly, manage bankroll, and trade across multiple platforms.

## Path 2: Standard (6 months, ~400 hours)

**Goal:** Build a solid, well-rounded prediction market trading practice. Covers all beginner and intermediate domains plus core advanced topics.

### Month 1: Foundations (~65h)
- **Probability & Bayesian Reasoning** (full domain, 45h)
- **Prediction Market Mechanics** (full domain, 19h)
- Milestone: Fluent in probability, Bayes, odds conversion; active on 2+ platforms

### Month 2: Financial Literacy & Forecasting (~70h)
- **Financial Markets Fundamentals** (full domain, 39h)
- **Forecasting & Calibration** (Phases 1-3, 30h)
- Milestone: Understand market microstructure; calibration within 10% of diagonal

### Month 3: Pricing & Information Edge (~75h)
- **Forecasting & Calibration** (Phases 4-5, 22h)
- **Pricing Theory** (full domain, 44h)
- **Information Sources** (core concepts, 10h)
- Milestone: Can price binary contracts, identify overround, detect mispricings

### Month 4: Risk & Position Management (~60h)
- **Information Sources** (full domain, 20h)
- **Risk Management** (full domain, 32h)
- Paper trading portfolio with Kelly sizing (8h)
- Milestone: Complete position sizing system; risk of ruin < 1%

### Month 5: Quantitative Methods (~70h)
- **Quantitative Modeling** (Phases 1-3, 70h)
- Milestone: Working probability model for one event category

### Month 6: Portfolio & Live Trading (~60h)
- **Quantitative Modeling** (Phase 4, 20h)
- **Portfolio Construction** (Phases 1-3, 40h)
- Live portfolio: 10+ diversified positions
- Milestone: Diversified portfolio with documented Sharpe ratio

**Outcome:** Systematic trader with quantitative models, calibrated forecasting, proper risk management, and a diversified portfolio.

## Path 4: Expert Specialization Tracks (After Path 2 or 3)

After completing a standard or comprehensive path, specialize in one of these tracks:

### Track A: Quantitative/Algorithmic Trader (~200h additional)
Focus: Domains 8, 13, 15
- Build end-to-end automated trading system

- Production deployment with monitoring and circuit breakers
- Multi-strategy capital allocation

### Track B: Market Maker (~150h additional)

### Track C: Event-Driven Specialist (~120h additional)
Focus: Domains 12, 6, 14

## Critical Learning Path (Shortest Path to Competence)

For someone who needs to start trading with positive expected value as fast as possible:

```
Probability Essentials (10h) → Platform Mechanics (5h) → Calibration Basics (10h)
→ Fair Value & Implied Probability (5h) → Kelly Criterion (5h)
→ Research Workflow (5h) → Live Trading with Journal (10h)
Total: ~50 hours over 4-5 weeks
```

This minimum viable path gets you:
- Ability to calculate fair value and identify mispricing
- Proper position sizing with fractional Kelly
- Basic calibration practice
- Systematic research approach
- Active trading journal for continuous improvement

**Warning:** This path sacrifices depth for speed. You will have gaps in quantitative modeling, portfolio construction, and advanced strategies. Plan to fill those gaps as you gain experience.

---

## Domain Sequencing Rules

1. **Never skip beginner domains.** Domains 1-3 are foundation for everything.
2. **Forecasting before Pricing.** Calibration quality determines pricing accuracy.
3. **Risk Management before any Advanced domain.** Without position sizing, good analysis produces bad outcomes.
4. **Quantitative Modeling before Algorithmic Systems.** Code without models is just infrastructure.
5. **Behavioral Edge before Strategy Integration.** Understanding your own biases prevents portfolio-level mistakes.
